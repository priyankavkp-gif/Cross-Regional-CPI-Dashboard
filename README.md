**Cross-Regional Consumer Price Index (CPI) Dashboard**

**Project Overview:**

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of U.S. and regional inflation trends using Consumer Price Index (CPI) data.

**The dashboard enables users to:**

Monitor national inflation trends
Compare inflation performance across metropolitan regions
Identify category-level inflation drivers
Analyze inflation volatility and risk
Explore future CPI trends through forecasting

**Dashboard Pages:**<br><br>
**1. Executive Inflation Overview**<br>
  High-level KPI's including:
- Current U.S. CPI
- Current U.S. YoY Inflation
- Selected Metro CPI & YoY
- Highest Inflation Metro Area
- Historical Inflation Trend Analysis

**2. National Inflation Drivers & Root Cause Analysis**<br>
  Analyze inflation contributors across categories such as:
- Energy
- Shelter
- Transportation
- Food
- Medical Care

**3. National Inflation & Volatility Explorer**<br>
  Understand inflation stability using:

- Standard Deviation
- Coefficient of Variation (COV)
- Historical Volatility Trends
- Category Comparisons

**4. CPI Forecast & Future Outlook**<br>
Forecast future inflation behavior using historical CPI data and trend analysis.

***Key Features***
